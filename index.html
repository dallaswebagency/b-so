Children in Japan

Did you know?

Starting at an early age, usually Grade 1, students in Japan learn to clean their own spaces, including the classroom, hallway and bathroom. It is a wholistic approach in Japanese education that teaches discipline, appreciation and teamwork rather than just the physical work of cleaning, helping them develop life skills.
